Receiptery — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026 · Applies to the Receiptery iOS app ("the App").

Summary

Receiptery is designed so that we do not collect any of your data. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking in the App.

1. Data Receiptery does not collect

  • We do not collect, transmit, or store your receipts, receipts data, purchase information, location, contacts, device identifiers, or usage analytics on any server operated by us.
  • Receipt scanning (OCR), data extraction, and storage happen on your device.
  • The App contains no third-party SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking technologies.

2. iCloud (Apple CloudKit) sync

If you enable iCloud sync, your receipts and settings are stored in your own private iCloud database operated by Apple. This data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We (the developer) have no ability to read your iCloud data.

3. Camera access

The App requests camera access solely to photograph paper receipts. Photos are processed on your device and saved only to your local library / your iCloud account as described above.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

Receiptery Premium is an optional auto-renewing subscription sold exclusively through Apple's in-app purchase system. Payments, billing details, and transaction records are handled entirely by Apple under its terms and privacy policy. We do not receive your name, payment details, or purchase records — Apple may show us aggregate sales reporting only.

5. Children

The App contains no content inappropriate for children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date. Changes to data practices will not be applied retroactively without your consent.
